Logo concepts

Six directions for the Stream Lab mark. The recommended one is applied across both canvases.

streamlab
Serpentine SRecommended

A stream folding back on itself forms an S, ending in one event. Reads as "flow" and as the letter. Simple at 16 px.

Watermark

Three streams and one vertical line: events before the watermark are solid, after it faded. Very Flink, a little abstract.

Window

Brackets of a window holding events. Clear meaning for Flink people; generic to others.

Tumbling tiles

Three windows stepping down. Friendly and modular, but reads more "design tool" than "stream".

Lens

A lens over a wave: "look inside the stream". Nice idea; close to many analytics logos.

Spark line

A live metric line with its latest point. Strong for the desktop app, weak for teaching.
