Rules

Say what happened, why (if known), and what to do, in that order.

Name the exact thing: the field, the line, the job, the node.

Never only colour: every state has an icon and words.

Keep the user's work: errors never clear a form or the editor.

One error = one place. Do not show the same error as toast and banner at once.

Log the technical detail behind "Copy details", never in the main message.

Disabled buttons explain why on hover and to screen readers.
